Three Distinct Studios

Designed for three distinct ways of storytelling.

Eva is not just a single generic chat window. It offers three dedicated environments tailored for free creative sandbox conversations, canon anime immersion, and deep living fictional universes.

Chatbot Studio

The Open Sandbox

Invent characters entirely from scratch. Define deep psychological profiles, mannerisms, tone, and system prompts. Enjoy pure, free-form creative roleplay with no canon constraints.

  • ✦ Complete character builder with 12 metadata dimensions
  • ✦ AI-assisted profile generator & single-section regeneration
  • ✦ Custom avatars, colors, voice tags, and opening greetings

Anime Studio

Canon Cast, Your Story

Converse with beloved canon characters from Naruto, High School DxD, and more. Non-destructive trait tuning lets you shape relationships and alter storylines while preserving their authentic personality and voice.

  • ❀ Rich library of canon anime presets ready for 1-click chat
  • ❀ Non-destructive traits: preserve canon lore while exploring what-ifs
  • ❀ Authentic character voices, mannerisms, and canon battle techniques

World Studio

Living Fictional Universes

Build an entire universe with its own lore bible, rival factions, geographic locations, and cast of NPCs. Cast yourself as protagonist or observer — your actions affect real-time reputation scores and world history.

  • ❖ Full world bible: factions, locations, laws, and history
  • ❖ Living NPC reputation engine (−100 to +100 dynamic scale)
  • ❖ Persistent timeline of world events and consequences
Engineering Quality

Built for serious conversation, deep privacy, and total control.

Every element of Eva is designed to put you in control of your data, your models, and your creative experience.

AI Character & World Generator with Online Lookup

Describe a character or world idea in plain English and Eva generates a rich, structured profile covering psychological dimensions, lore bibles, dialogue examples, and opening greetings. Integrated Wikipedia and Fandom lookup grounds canon personas accurately, and single-section regeneration lets you fine-tune specific fields without losing the rest.

✦ "Cyberpunk combat medic running an underground clinic" → Generates 12 distinct persona fields in seconds with granular section regeneration.

Scene Direction Command Syntax

Go beyond plain chat text. Eva's specialized parser gives you directorial control over dialogue, thought processes, environmental events, and NPC actions directly from the composer.

(thought) · "action" · (: other's thought) · "" : other's action · <god rewrite> · [world event]

Scene Visuals & Image Generation

Capture memorable conversation moments into striking scene illustrations using the Flux family (Flux Pro, Realism, Anime) via Pollinations or Imagen 3 via Google AI. Eva analyzes current chat context to offer 4–6 tailored visual scene suggestions, complete with strict safety pre-checks and persistent IndexedDB storage.

Pollinations Flux (Free) · Google Imagen 3 · Context-aware scene prompt suggestions · IndexedDB gallery

Synthetic Speech & Dialogue-Only TTS

Listen to spoken character dialogue with natural, expressive voice synthesis from Google AI (Puck, Kore, Charon) and Pollinations (Alloy, Echo). Eva's smart dialogue filter automatically isolates spoken dialogue from stage directions and parenthetical thoughts, speaking only what the character says aloud.

Dialogue filter (skips thoughts/actions) · Google AI voices (Puck) · Pollinations audio · Per-chat voice overrides

Hardware-Backed Security & Local Privacy

All conversations, characters, and worlds stay securely on your local device. API keys and credentials are encrypted using platform-native hardware security (Android Keystore hardware-backed keys and Electron SafeStorage) with zero plaintext persistence.

Local Storage · Android Keystore AES · Electron SafeStorage · Zero Central Servers

Long-Term Memory & Living Reputations

Eva tracks ongoing relationship developments and crucial scene milestones. The long-term memory engine ensures characters remember promises, shared trauma, and evolving secrets, while the World Studio updates faction standing in real time.

Reputation: [-100 Hostile … 0 Neutral … +100 Adored] · Dynamic memory injection in prompts
